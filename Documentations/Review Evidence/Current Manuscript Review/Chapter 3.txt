P0001: Chapter 3
P0002: RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
P0003: This chapter discusses the methodology used to develop IronGrind: A Fitness Gym Membership and Operations Management System for Iron Grind Fitness Gym in Purok Mangga, Sulangon, Dapitan City, Zamboanga del Norte. IronGrind is a web application with an installable attendance-scanner component. This chapter describes the development process, system requirements, design, data organization, and evaluation procedures. The system organizes membership applications and records, membership periods and renewals, attendance, products, sales, reports, and activity review. Its development is organized using Agile, with work planned and delivered incrementally. Proposed evaluation activities are distinguished from results that have not yet been collected.         
P0004: System Development Methodology
P0005: Figure 1. Agile
P0006: Figure 1 should be read as a recurring development cycle, not as a one-time sequence. Planning identifies the workflow or requirement to address; design translates that requirement into pages, forms, data relationships, and access rules; development builds a selected increment; and testing and review determine whether that increment satisfies the stated requirement. Findings and feedback return to planning, so a later increment may refine an earlier design. The Launch stage means release only after the relevant increment has been verified. The figure organizes the project's membership, renewal, attendance, inventory, sales, and reporting work, but it does not claim use of a particular Agile framework, a fixed iteration length, completed evaluation results, or a production deployment.
        
P0007: Pavlović (2018) discusses the importance of organizational support, consistent practices, and adequate skills and training when adopting Agile. This perspective informed the choice of approach for the study and supports treating iterative development as a structured process requiring planning and review. The phases shown in Figure 1 organize development tasks for IronGrind, including its membership, attendance, inventory, sales, and reporting features.
P0008: Requirements Planning
P0009: Requirements planning identifies separate workflows for applicants, members, administrators, and authorized staff. Requirements cover online applications subject to authorized staff or administrator review, direct counter registration and membership changes, QR attendance, product inventory, transaction recording, reporting, staff accounts, phone pairing, and activity logs. Acceptance criteria include role permissions, required inputs, stock and membership updates, and rejection of unauthorized operations. These criteria guide each development increment.
P0010: User Design
P0011: Using the prioritized requirements, the team designs or refines the corresponding user workflows, page layouts, forms, reports, data relationships, and role permissions. For example, the design distinguishes pending applications from approved member records, keeps attendance as a separate operational record, and stores sales with their itemized lines. The architecture, use-case, and data-flow figures in this chapter summarize how these parts fit together. These designs are then represented in prototypes so that the intended workflows can be reviewed before or during construction.
P0012: Prototype
P0013: The prototype represents the designed workflows through browser-based pages. Applicants submit registration details, while members view their membership, attendance, QR code, profile, catalog, and renewal requests. Administrators view member profiles without editing personal details, review online requests, perform membership operations, and handle inventory, sales, staff access, phone pairing, activity logs, and reports. Staff use a separate portal for attendance, walk-in registration, direct membership renewals and active-member plan changes, product sales, their own product-sale records, and online registration and renewal request approval. The system records in-person transactions without providing receipts to customers. Supabase Auth, database policies, functions, and authorized Edge Functions support the implemented operations.
P0014: Construction
P0015: The team constructs the selected increment using the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript client and Supabase authentication and PostgreSQL services. Database policies and functions support authorization and operational transactions. The completed increment is tested against the acceptance criteria established during planning. If a test identifies an issue, the team corrects it and checks it again; unresolved or newly discovered needs are recorded for prioritization. Once the increment passes the required checks, it can be prepared in the intended environment and reviewed. Test findings and any feedback actually collected inform the next planning cycle, connecting construction to continued refinement.
P0016: Implementation
P0017: Implementation prepares verified increments for operational use. The current web client is hosted through Vercel. The project dashboard supplied for this review shows a Production Deployment marked Ready, served at irongrind-github-io.vercel.app and sourced from the main branch. Supabase provides authentication and PostgreSQL services. The Ready label reports Vercel deployment status; by itself, it does not verify every production workflow or establish that staff orientation and formal evaluation are complete. Those results should be reported after the corresponding activities have been performed.
P0018: Deployment status must be checked separately for subsequent revisions. The November 10, 2026 source review added server-audited member email sign-in, security-event functions and triggers, and corresponding interface changes. Deploy the security-activity migration and both the new member-password-login and updated admin-username-login Edge Functions before releasing the dependent web client. Password-reset-request capture requires database Auth audit storage. These local implementation changes and isolated tests do not establish that the production database, functions, and Vercel client already contain this revision.
P0019: Verification and Evaluation
P0020: Recorded verification as of November 8, 2026 comprised forty automated check groups with no failed groups in the saved regression report. A check group may contain multiple assertions. The checks covered JavaScript syntax, local page references and identifiers, form validation, account registration, role authorization, staff operations, session ownership, attendance request handling, phone pairing, and membership-payment updates. Browser logic used simulated document and service responses; selected registration checks used the Supabase browser SDK with mocked Auth responses.
P0021: Database behavior was tested in isolated PostgreSQL fixtures using PGlite. Scenarios included rejection of unauthorized operations, duplicate requests, transaction rollback, numeric membership-payment updates, separate dashboard and scanner sessions, device-specific pairing records, pairing expiry, and the combined paired-plus-pending slot limit. Functional test writes did not create transactions in the gym's production member records. The linked database lint check reported no errors after the corrections. The saved regression report and database lint result describe the tested code and configuration at that review date.
P0022: A separate performance evaluation remains necessary. The available regression checks do not establish a maximum number of concurrent users, a load-response-time target, or a throughput guarantee. A controlled staging test should exercise logins, dashboard reads, registrations, and simultaneous attendance scans while recording response-time distributions, error rates, and database behavior. The reported user capacity should be based on those measurements and the actual hosting configuration, consistent with the production-testing guidance discussed in Chapter 2.
P0023: Successful physical multi-phone operation and complete visual browser behavior require separate recorded validation. The available automated artifacts do not establish completion of that end-to-end validation. The prototype figures document supplied browser-interface captures and role-specific controls; they do not, by themselves, establish the current production deployment state, successful physical camera operation, or measured usability. Formal usability or stakeholder-evaluation results should be reported only after the corresponding evaluation has been conducted and documented.

P0161: Data Process Modeling
P0162: The data-process model follows information from an actor, through an authorized system function, to the records it reads or changes. Applicant registration and renewal requests are reviewed before they create or update member and payment records. Attendance scans are checked against the member and any open visit before timestamps and operator identities are stored. Product sales read catalog and stock data, then save transaction lines and deduct stock. Reports and activity logs are separate read operations with different access rules.

For example, a member renewal begins as an authenticated request, enters the pending-request review process, and on approval updates the application, member dates, history, and transaction records. The diagrams describe the documented design; the workflow explanations and data dictionary identify its implementation boundaries and current limits.

P0171: Context Diagram
P0173: Figure 2. Context - Gane-Sarson
P0175:              Figure 2 defines the external boundary of the IronGrind Membership and Operations Management System using a Gane-Sarson context diagram. Process 0 represents the system as a single unit, while the rectangles identify its three external user groups: Member / Applicant, Administrator, and Authorized Staff. Each directed arrow describes information entering or leaving the system. An arrow pointing toward Process 0 represents submitted data or a request; an arrow pointing toward an external entity represents information or a result returned to that user group. The diagram provides an overview of responsibilities and information exchange, rather than the sequence of screens, database operations, or implementation steps.                  
P0176:             The Member / Applicant entity combines two stages of the customer relationship: a person applying for membership and an existing member using personal account services. Its incoming flow consists of credentials, registration details, renewal requests, and permitted profile changes. Registration details describe the information submitted for review; submission alone does not mean that membership has been approved. A renewal request concerns an existing membership and must be reviewed before its result is applied. Permitted profile changes concern the signed-in member's own editable personal information and do not include authority to approve requests, alter product stock, record sales, or modify another member's account.                  
P0177:            The corresponding outgoing flow returns sign-in and application results, the member's own membership information and QR code, attendance history, and the active product catalog. Access results indicate whether account access is permitted, while application results communicate the recorded review status. Membership information enables the member to check the plan, status, and applicable membership dates. The QR code supports identification during an authorized operator's attendance scan; showing the QR code does not give the member attendance-recording privileges. Attendance history is limited to the member's own visits. The catalog allows products to be viewed, while product-sale entries are handled through the administrator or authorized staff workflow rather than through a member checkout process.                  
P0178: The Administrator entity exchanges credentials, application and renewal review decisions, membership operations, product and stock updates, product-sale entries, staff-account controls, phone-pairing controls, and report or activity-log queries. These are separate authorized requests rather than one combined transaction. Phone-pairing controls concern the attendance camera connection only. Website trusted-browser and authenticator verification are a separate website-login security workflow; they neither authorize nor replace the attendance scanner's independent operator session. Queries retrieve information; they do not themselves change the underlying business records.
P0179: The system returns administrator access results, operation confirmations, member and staff information, phone-pairing states, reports, and recorded activity. The return flow lets the administrator confirm a submitted operation or inspect information within the permitted scope. Viewing an existing member personal profile remains read-only. Membership approval, renewal, and plan changes are operational responsibilities and do not authorize editing that member's personal profile. The scanner's password-based operator session is deliberately separate from the administrator's website trusted-browser session, so a scanner sign-in does not silently transfer or revoke website access.
P0180:          The Authorized Staff entity exchanges credentials, counter details, request-review decisions, QR scans, and product-sale entries. After staff identity and active-account status have been verified, the permitted tasks include walk-in member registration, limited member lookup for counter transactions, direct renewal, active-member plan changes, review of online registration and renewal requests, attendance scanning, and product-sale recording. A member lookup supplies the limited details needed to select the correct person for an authorized transaction; it is not general access to the member's personal profile. Staff review decisions are based on the request information made available by the staff interface. The QR scan flow submits a member identifier for the system to validate and record the applicable attendance result.                  
P0181:           The system returns staff access results, pending-request information or status, counter-operation results, scan results, permitted attendance records, and the staff operator's own product-sale records. These outputs support the assigned task and allow its result to be checked. Staff access does not include viewing or editing existing member profiles, maintaining inventory, administering staff accounts or phone pairings, or opening administrator reports and activity logs. The narrower staff outputs distinguish the staff workspace from the administrator workspace even when both roles can initiate a supported membership or attendance operation. The diagram represents these information exchanges; authentication, role checks, database policies, and authorized functions enforce the access boundaries in the implementation.                  
P0182:         Walk-in customers are not drawn as a separate direct system user because an administrator or authorized staff operator enters their counter transactions. The customer supplies the relevant details to that operator, who submits the supported system entry and receives its confirmation. A saved sale or membership transaction is an internal operational record; the confirmation does not establish that the system issues a customer receipt. Likewise, the context diagram's arrows represent information exchange rather than a physical payment channel, an online payment gateway, or the transfer of goods.

P0192: 1.0 Authenticate and Authorize
P0193: Supabase Auth verifies credentials and establishes the user session. The system reads the profile role and active-account state to route the account to the administrator, staff, or member interface. Authorization is enforced through database policies, role-checked functions, and Edge Functions, rather than relying on page visibility alone.
P0194: 2.0 Review Online Applications. Applicants submit registration details and health declarations to membership_applications. Authorized staff and administrators review online requests and approve or reject them. Approval creates or links the member record, updates the application status, and records the applicable membership payment through sales and sale_items. Direct walk-in registration follows process 3.0 as a separate counter operation.
P0195: 3.0 User Management. Administrators can view member profiles but cannot edit their personal information. They perform authorized membership operations and create or activate/deactivate staff accounts through authorized services. Members can retrieve and update only their own permitted personal details. Staff have a separate labeled counter flow: walk-in registration, limited member lookup, direct renewal, and active-member plan change. Staff cannot view or edit existing member profiles. Account rows created by the registration service describe backend storage, not profile-editing permission granted to staff. Membership changes record applicable member, history, sales, and sale-item updates.
P0196: 4.0 Review Online Renewals. Members submit renewal requests to membership_applications. Authorized staff and administrators approve or reject these requests. An approved renewal preserves unused time by extending an active membership from its current expiry date; an expired membership restarts on approval. Approval updates the member, history, application status, and membership transaction records. Direct counter renewals by an administrator or staff operator belong to process 3.0 and are separate from the online Requests approval workflow.
P0197: 5.0 Record and View Attendance. An authorized administrator or staff operator scans a member QR code with a supported computer or phone camera. The operator signs in to the attendance scanner using the same authorized account credentials, but the scanner maintains its own password-based attendance session; it does not require or use the website administrator authenticator or trusted-browser proof. When another scanner requests the same operator session, the currently active scanner is asked to approve or deny that handoff. The database validates the operator and member, checks for an open visit, and records check-in or check-out in attendance. recorded_by and checked_out_by identify the operators for each event. Private attendance scan requests and scanner-session records support duplicate protection and the scanner handoff. Paired phones use administrator-managed pairing sessions and policy; pairing connects the phone camera to the attendance workflow but does not replace operator authentication.
        
        
        
        
P0198: Phone pairing capacity counts both paired devices and unexpired pending QR reservations. At the initial two-slot limit, the permitted combinations are two paired phones, one paired phone plus one pending QR, or two pending QRs. Scanning a reserved QR completes that reservation without allocating another slot. Creating a further QR is blocked at capacity, while an existing pending QR remains available to scan. Each browser installation has its own identifier; another installation cannot take over an assigned QR. The device label is descriptive information and does not provide hardware attestation. An expired, removed, or unsuccessful pairing is not counted as a completed device connection.
P0199: 6.0 Manage Inventory and Catalog. Administrators maintain product details, prices, stock, product forms, and active or archived state. Powder supplements support weight units; drinkable supplements support volume units; capsules and tablets use pieces or boxes; other supplements and merchandise use items. Sale processing converts compatible quantities and deducts stock using the configured inventory units. Members browse active products, and staff obtain the permitted catalog needed for sales; these read operations do not grant inventory-maintenance permission.
P0200: 7.0 Sales
P0201: Administrators and staff record in-person product purchases for members or walk-in customers. The sale operation validates quantities and product status, writes sales and sale_items, and deducts product stock. Membership charges are recorded through the membership operations in processes 2.0 to 4.0. The database retains the existing receipt_number field as an internal transaction identifier. The documented operating process does not provide customers with receipts.
P0202: 8.0 Generate Reports
P0203: Administrators select a report type and period to retrieve the relevant operational records. Supported outputs include preview, print layout, individual PDFs, and combined ZIP export. This reporting process does not manage staff access, phone pairings, or activity-log filters.
P0204: 9.0 Activity Logs. 
P0205:             Administrators review database-recorded actions using date, account, action, and area filters and pagination. Logs include recorded staff-account changes and phone-pairing events. The interface reads activity_logs; it does not create staff accounts, alter pairing settings, or modify the recorded events. Those operations occur in User Management or QR Attendance and produce their own log entries.

They do not give staff general access to member profiles, inventory maintenance, staff-account administration, phone-pairing administration, administrator reports, or activity logs. Administrator is associated with all nine functions and performs the operational and supervisory actions permitted by the administrator workflow. Its broader participation still respects the implemented restriction that member personal profiles are viewed without editing their personal details.
P0213: User Login verifies an account and establishes access for its role and active-account state. For website administrators, this function covers trusted-browser proof, initial TOTP registration, replacement-browser approval, fresh TOTP before transfer, and explicit trust revocation. Both approving and requesting website sessions represent the same Administrator actor; they do not create new organizational roles. The attendance scanner is intentionally different: it accepts the same authorized administrator or staff credentials into a separate, password-based scanner session, without website TOTP or trusted-browser verification. Scanner handoff approval is requested from the first active scanner only, and does not log out or alter the user's website dashboard session. Figure 4 retains nine broad function ovals because these are authentication scenarios within the existing role workflows, not separate business actors.
P0214:           Membership Application covers online registration submission and its review. The applicant supplies the required registration information and receives the recorded application status. An authorized staff member or administrator reviews the limited pending-request details and records an approval or rejection. Approval is performed through an authorized server-side operation that rechecks the operator and pending state, prevents repeat processing, and creates or links the appropriate member and membership transaction records. The applicant's association represents submitting and checking the request, while the operator associations represent review and processing. They are different responsibilities within one functional area, not interchangeable permissions.
P0215:           User Management includes role-specific account, personal-profile, and counter membership responsibilities. Administrators view member profiles without editing their personal details and control supported staff-account access. Members alone update their own permitted personal-profile details. Staff register walk-in members and process direct renewals or active-plan changes using limited lookup information. Those counter operations create or update the records required for the authorized membership transaction; they do not provide a general member-profile viewer or an existing-profile editing function. All three User Management associations are therefore intentional and match the functional requirement. The administrator and member associations must not be interpreted as granting the same personal-information editing rights.
P0216:          Renewals allows a member to submit an online renewal request and obtain its status, while authorized staff and administrators review and approve or reject the request. Approval updates the linked membership, records the applicable renewal history and transaction, and saves the review decision. The online request workflow is distinct from a direct counter renewal, which is described under the operator responsibilities of User Management. Both workflows concern membership continuation, but the broad Renewals association does not let a member approve a request or directly change the recorded membership dates.
P0217: QR Attendance has different member and operator uses. Members display their own QR code and view their own attendance history. Authorized operators scan member QRs, while staff may alternatively search by name or Gmail, select the correct matching member and explicitly record attendance. The selected unique member identifier is submitted to the attendance service, avoiding reliance on potentially shared names. A search does not itself record a visit or grant profile-viewing rights. The service checks the member and current visit state before recording a check-in or check-out with its timestamp and operator. Staff feedback identifies the member, outcome and Philippine display time; the database timestamp remains the saved event time. Administrator-managed phone pairing is separate from operator sign-in and installation of the scanner PWA. Members cannot record their own attendance.
P0218:            Product & Inventory combines product viewing with administrator maintenance. Members browse the active product catalog, and staff read the product information permitted for their sales tasks. Administrators maintain supported product details, prices, units, stock, and active state. All three actors are connected because they participate in this functional area, but only the administrator maintains inventory. Catalog access does not establish an online member checkout, authorize a stock adjustment, or permit a staff member to change product information.
P0219:            Sales is associated with Administrator and Authorized Staff. Authorized operators enter the relevant customer and product-sale details, while the system validates the item, sale unit, quantity, price, and available stock, records the sale and line items, and applies the corresponding stock deduction. Staff may review their own product-sale records; the association does not imply unrestricted access to other operators' sales. Members are not connected to Sales because the implemented workflow records transactions through an operator. A saved system transaction or its internal receipt_number reference does not establish that the system issues a customer receipt.
P0220:            Generate Reports and Activity Logs are administrator-only use cases. Generate Reports retrieves supported operational information for the selected report and its available preview or export. Activity Logs retrieves recorded events using the supported filters and paging controls. Neither function is associated with Member / Applicant or Authorized Staff. Reading an event in the log is also distinct from performing the action described by that event; the log viewer does not execute membership changes, inventory changes, account changes, or pairing controls.

Figure 4 identifies actor participation and functional scope, while the DFD identifies the information exchanged with processes and stores. The data dictionary and relationship descriptions document the persistent records affected by the authorized operations. Their shared function names support consistency across the chapter, but a diagram association is not an access-control mechanism. Database policies, active-role checks, authorized functions, and server-side services enforce the restrictions described here. The system-flow discussion that follows explains how these responsibilities operate together in the implemented workflows.
P0222: End-to-end system flow. Each protected operation begins with authentication and an active-role check. Page visibility alone does not authorize a database change; database policies, role-checked functions, or authorized server-side services validate privileged operations.
P0223: 1. Applicant account and request. A prospective member creates an account with email and password through Supabase Auth before the online application is reviewed. The application is stored separately with its applicant account link and a pending status. Submission alone does not create an approved member record or active membership.
P0224: 2. Review and membership activation. Authorized staff or an administrator loads limited pending-request details, confirms that payment was received at the gym, and chooses approve or reject. The server rechecks the active role, locks the pending request, and prevents a second review. Rejection records the decision. Approval of registration creates the member record linked to the existing Auth account; approval of renewal updates the membership dates. Approved requests also record the membership transaction, and renewals add a membership-history event.
P0225: 3. Member self-service. A member signs in with email to view their own membership, progress, QR code, attendance history, and active catalog. They may update permitted personal details and submit a renewal request. A member can access only their own records; administrator profile viewing is read-only, and staff have no member-profile viewing or editing permission.
P0226: 4. Counter and attendance operations. Administrator and staff walk-in registration collect first name, optional middle initial and last name, combine them into the existing full-name value, and submit the membership details to an authorized service. Direct renewal and active-plan change use limited lookup rather than a profile screen; staff confirm the selected member, current plan, payment amount and replacement dates before saving. Attendance uses a member QR or staff name/Gmail lookup with explicit selection. The service checks the member and open visit before recording check-in/out and the responsible operator. An earlier-date visit without check-out is shown as Incomplete without inventing an exit time. Phone pairing, operator authentication and PWA installation are separate steps.
P0227: 5. Products, sales, and oversight. Administrators maintain the catalog, prices, product units, stock, and active state. Members and staff read only the catalog permitted for their role. When an operator records an in-person product sale, the service validates the item, unit, and available stock, saves the sale and line item, and deducts the quantity. IronGrind records payment at the gym but does not issue a customer receipt. Staff may review their own product sales. Administrators separately generate reports and read filtered activity logs; the log viewer does not perform the logged operations.
P0228: 1. Authenticate Account
P0229: Supabase Auth verifies credentials. Members sign in with the email address on their account; staff and administrators use their assigned usernames. Member registration collects an email and password, not a separate username, and the member dashboard displays the member's full name. The profile role and active-account state determine administrator, staff, or member access, with protected operations checked by server-side authorization. New member registration uses a visible password-strength indicator and blocks passwords that do not meet the Strong requirements: 8 to 128 characters, uppercase and lowercase letters, at least one digit, and at least one symbol. Confirmation must match the selected password. Auth and the member-creation service enforce the password policy on the server; staff-account creation uses a minimum of 12 characters with the same character requirements. Existing sign-in forms continue to accept account credentials without imposing registration validation on login.
P0230: Administrator device authorization is part of User Login rather than an additional main function. Password authentication establishes an Auth session but does not by itself authorize an administrator dashboard or scanner session. The server checks the active role, application scope, current trusted public key and authorized session mapping. A session may access protected operations only after the applicable device-verification workflow completes. Staff and member login retain their role-specific checks.         
P0231: First registration and return visits. The genuine administrator privately enrolls a TOTP authenticator when required and verifies a fresh code to register the first trusted browser. The browser creates an ECDSA P-256 key pair and retains a non-extractable private CryptoKey in IndexedDB; the server stores the public JWK. Trust lasts thirty days from registration. A later password login in the same browser and origin proves possession of that key automatically, without requesting a code on every login. Routine reuse does not extend the trust period. This is browser-profile trust, not a hardware identity: a different browser, origin, cleared storage or unavailable key can require registration again.         
P0232: Replacement while another trusted session is active. A different browser submits a session-bound approval request after password verification. The currently active trusted browser receives an in-website notification and may approve or deny it. The requesting browser must then complete fresh TOTP verification and sign its server-issued challenge. Approval alone does not authorize access. A successful server transaction replaces the trusted key and revokes the previous dashboard and attendance scopes; cancellation, denial or failed verification preserves the previous trust. Requests expire after two minutes, and signing challenges after ten minutes.         
P0233: Availability, logout and recovery. The application polls for approval notifications while the trusted page is active; a heartbeat within two minutes determines whether an eligible approving session is recently active. This is not SMS, operating-system push, or proof that a physical device is online. When no eligible approving session is available, fresh authenticator verification provides the implemented replacement path. Normal logout retains browser trust, while Log out and forget this device revokes it. Lost authenticator access requires verified project-owner recovery. There is no password-only bypass or custom backup-code recovery. Device-signature authorization does not itself change the Auth JWT assurance level to AAL2.         
P0234: Implementation and evidence. admin-device-trust.js coordinates browser-key proof, admin-device-approval.js handles approval notifications, and admin-mfa.js performs enrollment and fresh authenticator verification. The admin-device-trust Edge Function validates the signed-in account and session, verifies the signature and fresh TOTP evidence, and calls service-only completion functions. Database policies and session guards enforce the result independently of visible dialogs. User-supplied CLI output confirms application of migrations through 20261110000004 and deployment of admin-device-trust. These outputs do not establish successful physical two-browser transfer, authenticator use or matching Vercel frontend deployment; those remain acceptance checks.         
P0235: Usernames and email addresses are checked for duplicates without regard to capitalization or surrounding spaces. Registration shows availability feedback and rejects identifiers already in use; server checks support this validation. Dashboard and attendance-scanner sessions are tracked as separate scopes. Ordinary replacement affects its own scope, while successful administrator trusted-browser transfer explicitly revokes both old scopes. Online pages check ownership about every five seconds and return to sign-in when replaced; offline or suspended pages check on resumption or reconnection. Trust transfer and explicit forget-device revocation are additional administrator security controls, not ordinary scope-only replacement.
P0236: 2. Register and Submit Membership Application. An applicant creates an account using an email address and password, then submits personal and health information for review by authorized staff or the administrator. Name fields accept names containing letters and reject numeric characters.
P0237: 3. Review Membership Applications. Authorized staff and the administrator review pending application summaries and approve or reject requests according to the gym's criteria, confirming in-person payment before approval.
P0238: 4. Create or Link Member Account. Approval by an authorized staff member or administrator links an online application to the approved member record. Direct walk-in registration by an authorized administrator or staff operator uses a server-side Edge Function to create the appropriate Auth, profile, member, and payment records.
P0239: 5. View Application status at Login. The User can check the current status of their membership application when signing in to see if it is pending, approved, or rejected.
P0240: 6. View Membership Dashboard. This function allows the User to access their personalized dashboard, displaying their active membership plan, time-based progress, and their unique QR code.
P0241: 7. User Management. The administrator can view member profiles but cannot update their personal details. Only members update their own permitted personal profile fields. Administrator membership operations, staff-account controls, and membership-history review are separate from personal-profile editing. Staff have no member-profile viewing or editing function.
P0242: 8. Record Membership Attendance. An administrator or authorized staff operator scans a member QR using a supported camera. Staff can also search by member name or Gmail, select the matching member and record attendance. The service validates the supplied member identifier and visit state, records check-in/out and the responsible operator, and returns the result.
P0243: 9. View Personal Attendance History. A member can view their own visit dates and recorded check-in and check-out times. A visit without a recorded check-out does not display an invented exit time.
P0244: 10. View and Update Own Profile. A member can view their own profile and update permitted personal details. The administrator may view member profiles but cannot edit them. Staff have no access to member profile viewing or editing.
P0245: 11. Browse Active Product Catalog. The User can view the available supplements and merchandise sold by the gym.
P0246: 12. Manage Products and Inventory. The Admin can add, update, and monitor the product catalog, ensuring that merchandise stock quantities and supplement details are accurate.
P0247: Low-stock policy and catalog organization. The administrator inventory view separates selling price from stock on hand and shows low-stock alerts in the configured stock unit. Powder alerts use 1,000 g for protein; 250 g for creatine, pre-workout, amino-acid and vitamin powders; and 500 g for other powders. Liquid alerts use 1,000 ml; individually sold capsules/tablets use 30 pieces; boxed supplements use four boxes; other supplement items use ten items; and merchandise uses four items per product/size. Product form and sale unit take precedence over supplement type. Pounds and ounces are converted before comparison, so the same physical quantity has the same alert status regardless of entry unit. Values at or below the threshold trigger an alert; zero stock is distinguished as unavailable. Thresholds are starting policy values in application code, not stored threshold columns or demand-based reorder forecasts. Staff catalog sections separate Supplements into Powders, Capsules, Tablets, Liquids/drinks and Other, with Merchandise separately. Saved form metadata determines grouping; legacy gram or milliliter products can use a unit-based fallback, while an unidentified form remains Other.                           
P0248: 13. Record Product Sale. An administrator or staff operator records an in-person product purchase, with validated quantities, saved sales and line items, and corresponding stock deduction. Staff can review their own product-sale records. No receipt provision is included in the documented operating process.
P0249: 14. Submit Membership Renewal Request. This function allows a member to submit a renewal request at any time, subject to approval by authorized staff or the administrator. If approved while the membership is active, the new membership duration is added after the current expiry date. If approved after expiration, the renewed period begins on the approval date.
P0250: 15. View Dashboard and Operational Reports. The Admin can view a summarized dashboard showing active members, daily sales, and attendance, as well as access specific operational reports.
P0251: 16. Export Reports. This function allows the Admin to generate, download, or print detailed reports for record-keeping and business analysis.
P0252: 17. Activity Logs and Related Administration. The administrator reviews recorded activity through the Activity Logs use case. Staff-account changes are performed under User Management; phone-pairing changes are performed under QR Attendance administration. These operations generate log entries but are not performed inside the read-only log viewer. Business approval or expiry email notifications are not included as implemented use cases.
P0253: Expanded audit coverage and presentation. Operational writes continue to generate database audit events. The security extension adds server-recorded failed credential attempts and rate-limit rejections, member and scanner session events, successful password updates, recovery requests and report-export requests. Failed attempts are attributed to an unverified visitor rather than assumed to be the account owner. Password-change events identify the affected account without storing its password or hash in the activity log; the authenticated actor is used when available, otherwise the authentication service is shown. Recovery requests do not prove that the requester owns an account or that an email was delivered. Report export means a request, not verified browser-download completion. The administrator viewer keeps action labels distinct from descriptions and places technical identifiers and changed-field names inside expandable Details. Account, grouped category, action and Philippine date-range filters operate with server-side pagination. Searches and routine page views are not comprehensively logged. Database and Edge Function deployment, plus enabled Auth database audit storage for recovery requests, are required for this expanded coverage.

P1542: Forms
P1543: The system uses registration, profile, renewal, member, inventory, sales, staff-account, and phone-pairing forms. Members submit online registration or renewal information and maintain their permitted profile details. Administrators review online requests, maintain products and stock, and manage staff and pairing access. Authorized staff use counter forms for walk-in registration, direct renewal, active-member plan change, and product purchases, and use Requests approval for online registration and renewal decisions. Attendance uses camera scanning under an authorized operator account. Completed payments and purchases are recorded as transactions; receipts are not provided to customers. Required fields, validation, and server-side role checks support consistent data entry.
P1544: The Create Staff Account form collects first name, optional middle initial, and last name in separate inputs. It formats a supplied initial with a period and combines the parts into the existing full_name value; separate name columns are not introduced. The form also checks username and Gmail availability, phone format, password strength, and matching confirmation. Staff directory access and activation controls remain administrator-only. The Requests approval view separately presents pending online registration and renewal summaries for authorized staff and administrator decisions, with in-person payment confirmation before approval.
